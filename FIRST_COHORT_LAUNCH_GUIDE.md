# GDS Ticketing First-Cohort Launch Guide

## 1. Student approval flow
1. Student enrolls and completes Paystack checkout.
2. Payment is marked as `paid_pending_approval`.
3. Admin reviews the payment in the portal.
4. Admin clicks `Approve access`.
5. Enrollment status changes to `approved` and the student gains access to the learning portal.

## 2. WhatsApp welcome message

Your payment is confirmed and your enrollment is under review. Once approved, you will receive access to your student dashboard and learning portal.

## 3. Approval notification message
Hello {student_name}, your enrollment has been approved.

You can now log in to your dashboard and begin the training. Please complete your first lesson and check the learning path in the portal.

Support: +234 800 000 0000

## 4. Support escalation flow
- Students experiencing payment issues should check the dashboard and retry the checkout if needed.
- Students waiting for approval should remain in the dashboard and watch for the admin-approved access state.
- Students needing help should use the WhatsApp and email support channels listed in the portal.

## 5. Admin checklist for first cohort launch
- Verify payment list in the admin dashboard.
- Approve access for confirmed payments.
- Confirm the student receives support and onboarding guidance.
- Direct students to complete the first lesson and the early quiz.
- Review the student portal for access and support status after approval.
