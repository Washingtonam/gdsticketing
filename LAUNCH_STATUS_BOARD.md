## 2. Launch-readiness checklist

### Immediate owner actions
- [ ] Confirm first cohort list and expected enrollment count.
- [ ] Review student payments and approve the first 10–20 enrollments.
- [ ] Send welcome message immediately after approval.
- [ ] Share onboarding instructions and first lesson access details.

### Support and follow-up actions
- [ ] Monitor first-day student activity and enrollment questions.
- [ ] Check lesson completion by cohort after the first 48 hours.
- [ ] Review common blockers and update onboarding guidance if needed.
- [ ] Maintain a daily follow-up rhythm for the first 30 days.

### Risk watchlist
- [ ] Late payment confirmations causing delays in approval.
- [ ] Students expecting immediate access after payment.
- [ ] Lack of response to onboarding questions in the first cohort.
- [ ] Incomplete lesson completion if learners are not guided through the first session.

### Operational status
- Workflow readiness: Ready
- Product readiness: Ready

- Approval workflow: Ready
- Student support readiness: Ready
- Risk level: Low to moderate during first cohort rollout

### Recommended launch posture
The project should proceed with the first cohort using a supervised onboarding model. The platform is ready, but the first cohort should be treated as a guided launch rather than a fully hands-off rollout.

---

## 4. First 30-day execution plan

### Days 1–3
- Approve the initial students.
- Send welcome and onboarding communications.
- Confirm that each learner can access the first lessons.

### Days 4–10
- Review progress and completion rates.
- Follow up with students who have not started.
- Gather feedback on course clarity and onboarding experience.

### Days 11–30
- Adjust messaging based on student friction points.
- Expand support scripts and FAQs.
- Prepare the next intake wave with stronger conversion and onboarding support.
