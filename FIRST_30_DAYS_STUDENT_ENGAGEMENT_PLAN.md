# First 30 Days Student Engagement Plan

## Week 1: Onboarding and access
- Welcome message sent immediately after enrollment.
- Approval message sent once payment is verified.
- Student logs in and opens the dashboard.
- Student completes lesson 1 and the first quiz or practical task.
- Support team responds within 24 hours to any issue.

## Week 2: Learning momentum
- Encourage completion of module 1.
- Check progress in the dashboard.
- Ask for questions in WhatsApp or email if a lesson is stuck.
- Follow up with any student who has not started lesson 1.

## Week 3: Confidence building
- Continue guided tasks and practice activities.
- Review progress across modules.
- Encourage students to complete the assignment or guided practice for the week.
- Follow up with students who are inactive.

## Week 4: Review and next step
- Ask students to complete the final module activity.
- Confirm they understand the next step in the learning path.
- Encourage continuation into the next cohort or advanced course.

## Engagement KPIs
- Students who completed lesson 1: ____
- Students with approved access: ____
- Students who submitted their first quiz: ____
- Students who requested support: ____

## Owner note
Use the first 30 days to keep an active support rhythm and monitor completion before moving students to the next stage.
