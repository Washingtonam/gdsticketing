# Owner Daily Launch Checklist

## Daily workflow

### 1. Review payment queue
- Open the admin payment review screen.
- Check all enrollments in `paid_pending_approval`.
- Confirm each one matches the correct course and amount.
- Approve valid payments.

### 2. Review student support issues
- Check support messages and emails.
- Confirm whether the issue is payment-related or access-related.
- Resolve or escalate based on the SOP.

### 3. Confirm student onboarding
- Ensure each approved student can log in to the dashboard.
- Confirm the course is visible in the student portal.
- Make sure the student sees the onboarding message and support instructions.

### 4. Monitor first-cohort milestone completion
- Ask students to complete their first lesson.
- Check that course progress is being tracked.
- Confirm quiz submission and module completion progress.

### 5. Owner escalation checklist
Escalate if:
- a payment is confirmed but not appearing in the queue
- a student is approved but cannot access the course
- there is a repeated checkout or funding issue
- support requests are not being answered within the target time
