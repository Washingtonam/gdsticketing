# First Cohort Follow-Up Checklist

## Student onboarding follow-up

### After payment is made
- Confirm the student received the welcome message.
- Confirm the payment appears in the admin queue.
- Confirm the student is waiting for approval.

### After approval
- Send the approval message immediately.
- Tell the student to open the dashboard and start the course.
- Ask them to complete the first lesson within 24 hours.

### First-week actions
- Check whether the student completed lesson 1.
- Confirm they can access the portal without errors.
- Review whether they submitted a quiz or assignment.
- Answer support questions within 24 hours.
