# Launch Summary for Owner

## Status
The platform is now ready for a first-cohort launch with:
- public course catalog
- secure Paystack payment flow
- admin approval workflow
- student dashboard with enrollment states
- student support and onboarding guidance
- final launch operations documentation

## What is working
- Students can browse the course catalog.
- Students can register and log in.
- Students can begin checkout and pay via Paystack.
- Student payments are tracked as `pending_payment` and `paid_pending_approval`.
- Admin can approve course access.
- Approved students can access the learning portal and course material.
- Dashboard and support messaging reflect the payment and approval status clearly.

## Ready for launch
- Payment flow is active.
- Course access approval is active.
- Support and onboarding instructions are in place.
- First-cohort launch materials have been prepared.

## Immediate next step
Run the first cohort with the approval script and support SOP, then monitor the first 10–20 enrollments and complete daily approval and follow-up actions.
