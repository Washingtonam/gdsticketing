# Final Launch Handoff

## Purpose
This document is the final operational handoff for the first cohort launch of the GDS Ticketing Academy.

## Core launch flow
1. Student enrolls and checks out via Paystack.
2. Enrollment is marked as `paid_pending_approval`.
3. Admin reviews the payment in the payment review page.
4. Admin approves access.
5. Student receives the approval message and dashboard access is unlocked.
6. Student begins lesson 1 and completes the first practical task.
7. Support team follows up for any access or onboarding issues.

## Owner responsibilities
- Review payment queue each day.
- Approve valid student enrollments.
- Send approval messages promptly.
- Monitor the first cohort for progress and access problems.
- Escalate unusual payment or access issues quickly.

## Support responsibilities
- Handle payment disputes and failed transactions.
- Reassure students while approval is pending.
- Guide students to the dashboard and first lesson after approval.
- Track questions and escalate unresolved issues.

## Launch performance checklist
- Payments are confirmed and approved on time.
- Students receive welcome and approval messages.
- Students can access the dashboard after approval.
- First lesson is completed within the first 24–48 hours.
- Support issues are resolved or escalated within 24 hours.

## Final note
This handoff package is intended to prepare the team for a clean and professional first cohort launch.
